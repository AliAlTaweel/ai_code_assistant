SkillsMatch MCP - example questions to try in the dashboard
=============================================================

Tip: switch role and model using the dropdowns in the top nav before sending.
Avoid "qwen2.5-coder:32b" (flagged red) - it hangs on this machine.
"gemma4:e4b" reliably chains multi-step tool calls; "llama3.1:8b" sometimes
stops after one tool call and fabricates the rest.

1) STAFFING MATCH (any role)
-----------------------------
Find me a senior Go engineer with at least 20 hours available per week
Who's available for React work, at least 15 hours a week?
I need an AI/ML engineer with PyTorch experience, 10+ hours available

2) MARGIN CHECK - allowed (role = ADMIN or FINANCE)
-----------------------------------------------------
What's the profit margin if we bill Alice Chen at $170 an hour?
What's the margin on Ben Osei at a $150/hr bill rate?
Compute the profit margin for Carla Reyes at $200/hour

3) MARGIN CHECK - denied (role = CONSULTANT or RESOURCING_MANAGER)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Same question as above - should come back as a permission denial
(shown live in the Trace tab as a "permission_denied" event) instead
of a number.

4) DRAFT AN ASSIGNMENT - allowed (role = RESOURCING_MANAGER or ADMIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assign Alice Chen to project X for 15 hours a week
Assign Ben Osei to the React project for 20 hours

Check the Approvals tab afterward - it creates a pending action, not a
live booking. Approve / Reject / Modify it there.

5) DRAFT AN ASSIGNMENT - denied (role = CONSULTANT)
-------------------------------------------------------
Same request as above - should be blocked before the model is even
called (no LLM tool call in the trace at all).

6) GENERAL / OFF-TOPIC (any role)
-------------------------------------
What's the weather like today?
Tell me a joke

Should fall through to the general intent with no tool calls, and
shouldn't invent staffing or financial data.
